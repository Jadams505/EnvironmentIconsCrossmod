Environment Icons Crossmod adds compatibility between Environment Icons and many popular Terraria mods.

Currently supported mods:
[*] Calamity
[*] Calamity Vanities
[*] Mod of Redemption
[*] Macrocosm
[*] Terraria Origins
[*] Spirit Reforged
[*] Stars Above
[*] Thorium
[*] Secrets of the Shadows
[*] Ancient Awakened: Classic
[*] Ancients Awakened [BETA]


  Note that compatibility is loaded automatically for whichever supported mods are installed.
